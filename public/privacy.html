Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TORQ Business Solutions (“TORQ,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use TORQ Chat (the “Service”).

1. What TORQ Chat is

TORQ Chat is an AI consulting assistant that provides general business guidance through specialized consultants. Output is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional legal, financial, tax, medical, or other licensed advice.

2. Information we process

  • Chat content. Messages you send and the model’s replies are transmitted to the TORQ API so we can generate responses.
  • Session token. A short-lived session token is stored in your browser (local storage) so the Service can authenticate API requests. It is not a password and is not used to identify you across other TORQ products unless you later sign in to an account-based version of the Service.
  • Device-local history. Conversation history is stored on your device (browser local storage) so you can resume chats. TORQ does not require a user account for the local web client described in this policy.
  • Technical data. Standard request metadata (e.g., timestamps, approximate region from network routing, browser user-agent) may be processed by our hosting providers for security, rate limiting, and reliability.

3. How messages are processed (AI subprocessors)

When you chat, your prompts and conversation context are sent to the TORQ API. The TORQ API may forward content to Anthropic (or another AI model provider we configure) as a subprocessor solely to generate responses. We do not use your chat content to train public marketing models under our control.

Model providers process data under their own privacy terms and enterprise/API data-use policies. Do not submit secrets, passwords, payment card numbers, health records, or other highly sensitive personal data into chat.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use chat content for third-party advertising.
  • We do not require you to create an account for the local/web client covered by this policy (account features, if added later, will update this notice).

5. Storage and retention

  • On your device: chat history remains until you clear site data or delete conversations in the app.
  • On TORQ systems: request logs and operational telemetry may be retained for a limited period for security, abuse prevention, debugging, and legal compliance, then deleted or aggregated.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the Service, including keeping model API keys on the server (not in the browser bundle). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children’s privacy

TORQ Chat is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). Do not use the Service if you are under the applicable age.

8. Your choices

  • Clear browser storage to remove local history and session tokens.
  • Stop using the Service at any time.
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy (see Contact below).

9. International processing

Processing may occur in the country where you use the Service and in locations where TORQ or its subprocessors operate. Where required, we apply appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact TORQ Business Solutions via the channels listed on your TORQ engagement materials or product site, referencing “TORQ Chat Privacy.”
