2. What TORQ Chat is

TORQ Chat is an AI multi-consultant assistant for business questions (strategy, marketing, operations, finance framing, and more). Responses are general guidance only and are not legal, financial, tax, medical, or other licensed professional advice.

3. Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. v1 uses an anonymous session. History is stored on your device.
  • Where do my messages go? Messages are sent over HTTPS to the TORQ API so we can generate replies. The TORQ API may use Anthropic (or another configured model provider) as a subprocessor. See our Privacy Policy.
  • How do I clear my chats? Delete conversations in the app when available, or clear the site/app storage for TORQ Chat in your browser or iOS app settings.
  • Something failed mid-reply? Check your network connection and try again. If the service is rate-limited, wait briefly and retry. If problems continue, email support with the time of the error.
  • Microphone permission? Live voice is not part of TORQ Chat v1. You should not be prompted for microphone access.

5. App Store & safety

For App Store review, TORQ Chat does not require a demo login. Open the app and start chatting. Bundle ID: com.torq.chat.

If you believe you have found a security issue, email support with [Security] in the subject line. Do not include live secrets in public forums.
