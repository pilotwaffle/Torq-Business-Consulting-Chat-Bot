## 4. Description

*Max 4000 characters. Plain text; use line breaks. No HTML.*

### Primary description (recommended)

```text
TORQ Chat is a multi-consultant AI workspace for founders, operators, and SMB teams.

Instead of a single generic chatbot, TORQ Chat lets you switch specialized advisors—strategy, marketing, finance framing, operations, code, and more—so every answer is shaped for the problem you’re actually solving.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Chat with focused business personas powered by advanced AI
• Stream clear, structured replies for planning, GTM, ops, and product questions
• Keep conversation history on your device and pick up where you left off
• Export transcripts when you need to share notes with your team
• Work in light or dark mode on iPhone

BUILT FOR REAL OPERATOR WORK
Use TORQ Chat to pressure-test positioning, outline launches, frame financial questions, sketch processes, and unblock technical decisions—with language that stays practical and decision-oriented.

YOUR DATA & PRIVACY
• No account required to get started (anonymous session)
• Chat is processed securely through the TORQ service to generate responses
• Conversation history stays on your device in this version
• We do not use your chats for cross-app advertising or tracking

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER
TORQ Chat provides general business guidance only. It is not a substitute for licensed legal, financial, tax, medical, or other professional advice. Always consult a qualified professional for decisions with legal or financial consequences.

TORQ Chat is a product of TORQ Business Solutions.
```
