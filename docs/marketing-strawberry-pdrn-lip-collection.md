11. FAQ

Q: Will this actually make my lips bigger, like filler? A: No — this is a cosmetic lip care system, not a filler alternative or medical treatment. Our formulas are designed to visibly improve the appearance of lip fullness, smoothness, and hydration through skincare actives. Results are cosmetic and vary by individual; this is not a substitute for dermal filler.

Q: Why doesn't it tingle or burn like other lip plumpers? A: We intentionally formulated without irritants like cinnamon, capsaicin, or menthol, which create temporary swelling through mild inflammation. Instead, we use peptide and PDRN technology that supports the lip's appearance over time with consistent use — comfort-first, always.

Q: What is PDRN? A: PDRN (polynucleotide) is a skin-conditioning ingredient widely used in K-beauty formulations, known for its regenerative and hydrating properties. It's a popular ingredient in advanced Korean skincare, now formulated specifically for lip care.

Q: Can I use both products together? A: Yes — that's the intended Ritual. Lip Oil is designed for daytime wear (alone or over lipstick); Overnight Mask is designed for nighttime intensive treatment. Using both maximizes consistency of active ingredient exposure.

Q: How long until I see results? A: Hydration and comfort benefits are typically noticeable immediately. Visible fullness and smoothness improvements build with consistent use — we recommend evaluating results at the 2-4 week mark.

Q: Is this safe for sensitive lips? A: Our formulas are designed with barrier-supportive ingredients (ceramides, squalane, bisabolol) and are free from common irritants. As with any new product, we recommend patch testing if you have known sensitivities.

Q: Is the strawberry flavor artificial? A: We use a naturally-derived strawberry flavoring for a clean sensorial experience without artificial aftertaste.

Q: What does "Volufiline 3-5%" mean — which is it? A: Our formulation is currently finalizing at either 3% or 5% concentration, subject to final manufacturer specifications. We'll confirm the final percentage prior to launch and reflect it accurately on packaging/PDP.
